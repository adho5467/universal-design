Basic Strategy

Plan Your Moves

Fire Emblem battles take place on a grid. Each unit has different strengths, weapons, and movement abilities.

Check enemy ranges before moving and protect units with low health.

Beginner Tip

Save your game often and learn which weapons work best against different enemies.
